Tour Overview

Undertake the sacred Char Dham Yatra through Uttarakhand’s four revered shrines: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. This suggested 9 nights / 10 days route begins and ends in Haridwar, with overnight halts along the Himalayan circuit. The day-wise outline is a planning reference inspired by commonly published operator routes; final dates, route, accommodation, meals and inclusions must be confirmed with RS Tours & Travels.

Tour Itinerary

Suggested 9 Nights / 10 Days road itinerary based on commonly published Char Dham operator routes. Trek lengths, road timings, stay points and temple access are subject to weather, local administration and the final operator plan.

Day 1Haridwar – Barkot

Meet at Haridwar and drive to Barkot via Dehradun and Mussoorie. Check in and rest for the pilgrimage ahead.

Day 2Barkot – Yamunotri – Barkot

Drive to Janki Chatti and proceed on the Yamunotri trek (approximately 6 km one way). Visit Yamunotri Temple and return to Barkot. Pony or palki services, if available, are generally at additional cost.

Day 3Barkot – Uttarkashi

Travel to Uttarkashi. On arrival, check in and visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple if time permits.

Day 4Uttarkashi – Gangotri – Uttarkashi

Drive via the scenic Harsil valley to Gangotri for darshan. Return to Uttarkashi for the night.

Day 5Uttarkashi – Guptkashi

Proceed towards Guptkashi through the Himalayan valleys. This is a long mountain-road travel day; overnight at Guptkashi or nearby.

Day 6Guptkashi – Gaurikund – Kedarnath

Drive to Gaurikund and begin the Kedarnath trek (commonly around 16–18 km one way). Visit Kedarnath Temple and stay overnight near the shrine, subject to availability.

Day 7Kedarnath – Gaurikund – Guptkashi

Complete morning darshan if possible, trek back to Gaurikund and transfer to Guptkashi for an overnight stay.

Day 8Guptkashi – Badrinath

Drive to Badrinath via the mountain route. Visit Badrinath Temple subject to arrival time and temple schedule.

Day 9Badrinath – Rudraprayag

After darshan, proceed towards Rudraprayag. En-route sightseeing may include Joshimath or other stops depending on road and time conditions.

Day 10Rudraprayag – Rishikesh – Haridwar

Drive back towards Haridwar via Devprayag and Rishikesh. Tour concludes at Haridwar.
