Tour Itinerary

Day 1Kochi

Arrive at Kochi and meet our representative. Transfer to your hotel, check in and relax. Later, enjoy the local surroundings and an evening at leisure.

Day 2Kochi – Munnar

After breakfast, drive towards Munnar through scenic countryside, waterfalls and tea plantations. On arrival, check in to your hotel and enjoy the cool mountain atmosphere.

Day 3Munnar

Explore Munnar's beautiful hill country, tea gardens and viewpoints. Visit the scenic Echo Point and enjoy time together among the rolling green landscapes.

Day 4Munnar – Periyar (Thekkady)

Travel to Periyar, Thekkady. Check in and enjoy the natural beauty of the region. The evening is at leisure to explore the local market or relax.

Day 5Periyar – Kumarakom

Proceed to Kumarakom, set beside the beautiful Vembanad Lake. Check in and enjoy the peaceful backwater surroundings.

Day 6Kumarakom

Enjoy a relaxed day in Kumarakom. Experience the backwaters and visit the bird sanctuary, with time to enjoy the resort and the serene landscape.

Day 7Kumarakom – Departure from Kochi

After breakfast, check out and transfer to Kochi for your onward journey with wonderful memories of Kerala.
