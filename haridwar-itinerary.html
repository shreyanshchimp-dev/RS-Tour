Tour Overview

Discover North India on this 5 nights / 6 days group tour. Tour Itinerary

Suggested day-wise outline based on the destinations shown on the tour poster. Final order, travel times and hotel stops must be confirmed with RS Tours & Travels.

Day 1Delhi Arrival & Akshardham

Arrive in Delhi, visit Akshardham if time permits, and settle in for the journey.

Day 2Delhi – Haridwar

Travel to Haridwar. Visit Har Ki Pauri and enjoy the evening Ganga Aarti.

Day 3Dehradun – Mussoorie

Explore Dehradun and continue to Mussoorie for local sightseeing and an overnight stay.

Day 4Rishikesh

Visit Rishikesh, including the riverfront and well-known suspension bridges, then continue as arranged.

Day 5Mathura – Vrindavan – Gokul – Govardhan

Explore the Braj region’s temples and sacred sites, including Mathura, Vrindavan, Gokul and Govardhan.

Day 6Agra & Departure

Visit Agra and the Taj Mahal, then proceed for the onward departure as per the confirmed transport plan.
