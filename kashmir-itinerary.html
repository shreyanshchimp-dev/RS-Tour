Tour Itinerary

Suggested day-wise outline based on the destinations shown on the tour poster. Vaishnodevi involves a separate transfer; confirm the final routing and train schedule before booking.

Day 1Arrival in Srinagar

Arrive in Srinagar, transfer to the hotel or houseboat and enjoy a relaxed evening.

Day 2Srinagar Sightseeing

Explore Srinagar’s gardens, lakefront and local markets. A shikara ride can be planned subject to availability.

Day 3Sonmarg Excursion

Travel to Sonmarg to enjoy its alpine scenery and mountain views. Return or stay as per the confirmed plan.

Day 4Gulmarg Excursion

Visit Gulmarg and enjoy its meadows and mountain landscape. Optional activities are subject to weather and local operation.

Day 5Pahalgam

Travel to Pahalgam and explore the Lidder Valley and surrounding scenery.

Day 6Pahalgam – Srinagar

Enjoy more time in Pahalgam before returning to Srinagar.

Day 7Srinagar – Katra / Vaishnodevi

Proceed towards Katra for the Vaishnodevi visit. The travel sequence and overnight arrangement must be confirmed.

Day 8Departure

Complete the return transfer and departure as per the confirmed train schedule.
