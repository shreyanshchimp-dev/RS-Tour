Tour Itinerary

Suggested day-wise outline based on the destinations shown on the tour poster. The confirmed sequence and overnight stops may vary with the departure plan.

Day 1Arrival & Transfer to Amritsar

Arrive at the designated pickup point and proceed towards Amritsar. Overnight stay as per the final plan.

Day 2Amritsar – Dalhousie

Visit the Golden Temple and continue towards Dalhousie.

Day 3Khajjiar Excursion

Explore Khajjiar, known for its open meadow and mountain views. Return to Dalhousie.

Day 4Dalhousie – Manali

Travel through the Himalayan landscape towards Manali.

Day 5Manali Sightseeing

Explore Manali’s local attractions, temples and scenic surroundings.

Day 6Kullu Valley

Visit Kullu and enjoy the valley scenery and local experiences.

Day 7Manali – Shimla

Proceed towards Shimla, with scenic stops along the way as time allows.

Day 8Shimla & Departure

Enjoy a short Shimla visit before the planned return journey.
